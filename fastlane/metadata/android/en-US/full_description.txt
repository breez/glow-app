Glow is a self-custodial Bitcoin app built on the Lightning Network. Payments settle in seconds, and the keys stay on your device.

Sign in with a passkey. Glow derives your keys from a passkey held by your device or your password manager, so there is nothing to write down before you can start. Prefer a recovery phrase? You can restore with one at any time.

What you can do:

* Send and receive over Lightning in seconds
* Receive on-chain bitcoin and have it brought in for you
* Pay Lightning addresses, invoices, and QR codes
* Keep the people you pay often as contacts
* Hold part of your balance at a stable value
* Buy bitcoin through built-in providers
* Lock Glow behind a PIN, your fingerprint, or your face

Your keys never leave your device. Glow cannot hold your funds, freeze them, or reverse a payment you have made.

Built by Breez on the Breez Spark SDK.
